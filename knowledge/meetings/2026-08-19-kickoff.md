## TL;DR

- Scope confirmed at roughly 1/3 of the original SOW, an eight-week phase
  starting Monday, three two-week build sprints after an initial two-week
  discovery/prototype-alignment phase.
- Platform: iOS is the working assumption; Eric Rice confirmed on behalf of
  the client ("I would assume iOS is probably the best way forward"), so this
  reads as closed for now, not reopened.
- Reinvent remains the north star launch date on both sides; Matthew Kaplan
  says the client is "all aligned around that date."
- Client product direction: slim the MVP down, not add to it -- daily prompts
  stay client-curated (not Claude-generated) for now given mental-health/safety
  review by their program team; comments/reactions on walls stay minimal
  (reactions yes, open commenting deferred, toggleable per group); monetization
  hooks should be built into the infrastructure now even if not turned on at
  launch.
- Christina (client designer) will be assigned a subset of the hardest screens
  to fully design; Rob will replicate her theme across the remaining screens
  rather than waiting on a full redesign. A weekly ~20-30 min required sync
  between Rob and Christina was agreed.
- Working agreements: daily morning stand-ups, Wednesday-morning demo/handoff
  cadence (client agreed to Wednesday as proposed), lightweight readouts (demo
  over slide decks), JIRA confirmed for the board, code to start in the
  Bounteous repo with a later migration to the client's own repo, Google
  Drive confirmed as the file-sharing channel over the Microsoft ecosystem.

## Decisions

- Scope is ~1/3 of original SOW; eight-week engagement clock starts Monday
  (David Lawton).
- Single platform for this phase is iOS; client confirmed this is fine, no
  pivot requested (David Lawton proposed, Eric Rice confirmed).
- Engagement structure: two-week discovery/prototype-alignment phase followed
  by three two-week build sprints (David Lawton).
- Demo/handoff cadence set to Wednesdays, morning US time, to preserve India
  team overlap (David Lawton proposed; Eric Rice agreed: "if that's what you
  think works best, let's do it").
- Daily stand-ups, morning time block; Matthew Kaplan opts in to attend on the
  client side.
- Readouts stay lightweight (demo-and-move-on, not slide decks) -- Matthew
  Kaplan: "I think the latter. I think move fast, get to the demo."
- JIRA confirmed as the shared board tool (David Lawton asked, Eric Rice: "Yap").
- Code starts in a Bounteous-owned repo, migrates to the client's repo later
  (Rob Forshier II proposed, Eric Rice: "probably the latter").
- Google Drive confirmed as the document-sharing channel; anything outside the
  Microsoft ecosystem preferred (Eric Rice).
- Daily prompts stay client-curated by their program team (mental health
  professionals, educators) rather than Claude-generated for the client-facing
  daily prompt; Claude/agent assistance is scoped to drafting suggestions and
  validating prompts against the client's own curated set and research corpus,
  not generating final prompts unsupervised (Eric Rice, Martin Young, Rob
  Forshier II).
- Comments/reactions: reactions ship as a baseline feature; open commenting is
  deferred past MVP and, if built, should be a per-wall-host toggle rather than
  always-on (Matthew Kaplan, Eric Rice).
- Monetization: not freemium-forever; infrastructure/hooks for paywalls should
  be in place for App Store release even if not turned on at launch, with
  before/after and multi-variant paywall testing planned (Matthew Kaplan,
  David Lawton).
