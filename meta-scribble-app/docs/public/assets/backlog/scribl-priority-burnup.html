3. How to read this

Rank is the card's left-to-right position inside its band rectangle on the live board. The client did not number the cards, so rank is reading order, not a stack rank they signed.

Three states. Underway means work has started and there is nothing to use. Demonstrable means it works at a demo and is not finished. Complete means done as the client wrote it.

Blank Jira cells mean the epic was not filed when this was written. No key here is predicted.

Blank Epic cells mean no backlog item names that card. It needs a definition before it can be ranked.

Every delivery week comes from the milestone statements on the Timeline and Milestones page. Sprint 3 has no feature-level assignment anywhere, so nothing here claims one.

"Not stated" is not the same as "not inside the eight weeks". Reactions is the only row that reads it. The sprint 2 milestone says there are no reactions at week 6 and the sprint 3 milestone does not mention them either way, so the plan of record neither schedules them nor rules them out.

Two cards sit in a different band on the board than in the backlog. Analytics instrumentation is GREEN on the board and ORANGE in the backlog, which does not change its answer. Reactions is ORANGE on the board and GREEN in the backlog inside an epic that completes in week 8, which does change its answer. Both need settling in the room.
