# Knowledge wiki index

Master catalog of durable project knowledge for scribl. Pages are created and
maintained following the `wiki-ingestor` conventions (see
`.claude/agents/wiki-ingestor.md`). Edits to this index are additive or
surgical -- never a full regeneration.

Page types: tools, repos, patterns, concepts, sources, assessments. Meeting
transcripts are operational records under `knowledge/meetings/`, not wiki
knowledge -- with the deliberate exception of workshop retros, which are durable.
